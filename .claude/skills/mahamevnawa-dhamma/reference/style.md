# Teaching style

Observed on mahamevnawa.lk and in the children's series "පුංචි අපේ දහම් පාසල" on
mahamegha.lk. Follow it for all child-facing and teacher-facing text.

## Principles

- **Pure Buddha-word first.** The monastery's stated aim is to share "ඉතා නිර්මල වූ බුදු වදන්".
  Teach what the Buddha taught, then apply it to the child's life. Do not replace it with
  general self-help or psychology.
- **Clear, correct, simple language.** The site's own words: "නිරවුල් නිවැරදි සරළ බසින්".
  Use everyday spoken Sinhala, short sentences, no academic terms.
- **Open to everyone.** The founder's message says the Dhamma service has no divisions of
  race, language, colour or religion. Never write anything that looks down on any group.
- **Learn, remember, understand, live it, share it.** "ඉගෙන ගනිමු. ධාරණය කරගනිමු. තේරුම් ගනිමු.
  ඒ අනුව ජීවත් වෙමු. ලොවට බෙදා දෙමු." Each lesson should end in something the child does.

## Voice for children

- Open with a warm address: "පින්වත් දුවේ පුතේ," or "පින්වත් දරුවනේ,".
- Speak to the child directly and kindly: "ඔයාලා දන්නවනෙ…", "බලන්නකෝ…".
- Tie good action to merit and to both worlds: පින, මෙලොව පරලොව යහපත.
- Prefer a short story from the Buddha's time when the source gives one. Name the person
  and the place only if the source does.
- Give the reason, not only the rule. Say what good comes of it.

## Reverent wording

| Say | Do not say |
| --- | --- |
| බුදුරජාණන් වහන්සේ, භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ | බුදුන්, බුද්ධ, Buddha said |
| දේශනා කොට වදාළා, වදාළ සේක | කිව්වා, said |
| ස්වාමීන් වහන්සේ, තෙරුන් වහන්සේ | හාමුදුරුවෝ in formal text, monk |
| ශ්‍රී සද්ධර්මය, උතුම් ධර්මය | religion, philosophy |
| අතිපූජ්‍ය කිරිබත්ගොඩ ඤාණානන්ද ස්වාමීන් වහන්සේ | the name without the title |

## How a citation is shown

1. Reference, for example "ධම්මපදය 183" and the collection.
2. The Pali, exactly as on tripitaka.online.
3. The Sinhala translation, exactly as on tripitaka.online.
4. A link to the page.
5. The credit line: "සිංහල පරිවර්තනය: අතිපූජ්‍ය කිරිබත්ගොඩ ඤාණානන්ද ස්වාමීන් වහන්සේ".

Long prose may be cut only at the end of a sentence, and the cut is marked with "…".

## Things to avoid

- Mixing in teachings, stories or terms from other traditions or other teachers.
- Psychological or scientific framing presented as Dhamma.
- Fear as the main motive. The children's articles explain results gently.
- Claims about what a sutta "really means" beyond the translation given.
- Pictures of the Buddha drawn by us. Use a stupa, Bodhi tree, lotus or lamp instead.
- Haloes or rays around ordinary people. A soft glow is enough.
- Imagery from other religions, such as angels, devils or horns.
- Teacher notes written as psychology. Give a simple classroom activity and point to the
  Buddha-word shown on the page.
